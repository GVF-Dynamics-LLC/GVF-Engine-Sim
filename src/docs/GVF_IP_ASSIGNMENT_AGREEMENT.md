# INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY ASSIGNMENT AGREEMENT

This Intellectual Property Assignment Agreement ("Agreement") is executed as of August 21, 2026, by and between:

**ASSIGNOR:**  
TCPARTSDIRECT, LLC, a Florida Limited Liability Company, together with Antonio Gonzalez (Inventor).

**ASSIGNEE:**  
GVF DYNAMICS, LLC, a Florida Limited Liability Company and wholly owned subsidiary of TCPARTSDIRECT, LLC.

---

### RECITALS

WHEREAS, Assignor is the sole owner and inventor of certain intellectual property, inventions, source code, hardware designs, and patent applications relating to the Generalized Variability Field (GVF) dynamic bitline activation hardware core; and

WHEREAS, Assignee desires to acquire 100% of the right, title, and interest in and to said intellectual property, and Assignor desires to assign all such rights to Assignee.

---

### AGREEMENT

1. **Assignment of Intellectual Property:** Assignor hereby unconditionally sells, transfers, conveys, and assigns to Assignee all right, title, and interest worldwide in and to:
   * **USPTO Provisional Patent Application No.:** 64/134,522 ("Generalized Variability Field Hardware Core").
   * **SystemVerilog RTL Source Code:** Including `hardware/rtl/gvf_bitline_comparator.sv` and all associated testbenches.
   * **Software Source Code & Benchmarks:** All repositories, simulation scripts (`src/bench_nmnist.py`, `src/bench_real_dvs.py`), trade secrets, and empirical datasets.

2. **Power of Attorney:** Assignor authorizes Assignee and its designated Patent Attorney (including USPTO registered counsel) to record this Assignment with the United States Patent and Trademark Office (USPTO) and to prosecute all corresponding Non-Provisional and International utility applications directly under the name of **GVF DYNAMICS, LLC**.

3. **Consideration:** This assignment is made in exchange for $10.00 and other good and valuable consideration, including the issuance of 100% membership interest in GVF DYNAMICS, LLC to TCPARTSDIRECT, LLC.
